About

The Council Of Larry is a community-focused Discord server built for people to connect, communicate, and enjoy being part of an active and growing community. Our goal is to create a welcoming place where members can chat, share interests, participate in events, and make new connections with others. Recently, we expanded the community by adding a Minecraft server, giving members another way to interact, work together, and create new experiences. Whether you enjoy Minecraft, community events, discussions, or just hanging out, The Council Of Larry is a place where everyone can be involved and have fun. We are always working on improving the community with new features, activities, and opportunities for members to take part in something bigger.
